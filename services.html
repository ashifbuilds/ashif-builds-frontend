100%. Unlike platforms that charge monthly rent, you own all the files, the database, and the domain—you are not tied to anyone.

We handle everything—from applying for the gateway (Razorpay/Stripe) to integrating it directly into your checkout page so you can start collecting money immediately.

We will guide you on how to renew it. Since you have full control and cPanel access, you are never locked into our services.

Absolutely. We provide a simple admin dashboard where you can add, edit, or remove products in seconds without needing any coding skills.

We configure your store with proper meta tags, sitemaps, and speed optimization from day one, which are the fundamental pillars of local Google ranking.
