How secure is this WordPress website against hacking and data leaks?

Your store is protected by elite enterprise security architecture. We deploy industry-standard security frameworks like Wordfence Security Firewall and Solid Security Pro (formerly iThemes) to block brute force attacks, continuously scan malware, and enforce automated database backups alongside end-to-end SSL encryption protocol settings.

What exact legal documents are delivered inside the kit?

We manage the entire configuration for your business registration foundation. This includes filling and issuing your official legal Goods and Services Tax (GST) Certificate and your government Udyam Registration (URN) setup, ensuring your enterprise is completely compliant with corporate banking regulations in India.

How does the payment gateway and bank application work?

Once your business certificate is generated, we guide you through acquiring a zero-balance or premium Corporate Current Account. Simultaneously, we process api authorization profiles with top merchant layers such as Razorpay or PayU to let your store instantly settle secure customer credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking transactions.

What platform technologies are deployed for my E-Commerce store?

Your ecosystem is engineered using premium WordPress & WooCommerce cores, layered with clean custom UI/UX templates. It features deep cache optimization engines, a mobile-responsive framework matching Progressive Web App (PWA) guidelines, complete cPanel access configurations, and Cloudflare integration for optimized load times.

Is there a limit on the number of products I can list?

No. Because your website is built on a scalable WordPress and WooCommerce foundation with unlimited data structures, you can list an infinite number of products, configurations, variations, and categories without any extra fees or platform penalties.

Is there an expiration date for the hosting infrastructure?
